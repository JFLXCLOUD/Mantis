# Hopper mascot prompts

Tool: built-in `image_gen.imagegen`, with `transparent_background: true`. No CLI or external reference artwork used. Final output copied into `public/brand/hopper-mascot.png`.

## Initial generation

Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: transparent mascot illustration for Hopper, an open-source craft cutting design app.
Primary request: Create one original, beautifully designed friendly grasshopper mascot. Full body in a lively poised-to-hop stance, three-quarter side view facing right. Distinctive large bent hind legs, a long folded leaf-shaped wing/body, rounded head, two elegantly curved antennae, expressive dark eyes and a subtle smile. Insect anatomy stylized, uncluttered and intentional. A small raised front leg makes a welcoming gesture. Grown-up charm, confident and curious, suitable for creative software rather than a children's toy.
Style/medium: polished flat illustration with crisp vector-like contours, broad rounded organic shapes, very restrained two-tone shading, no texture. Main deep forest green #285c48, fresh sage #8fbc8f, pale mint #dce9d8, warm ivory #f5f8df. Dark green outlines only where useful. Beautiful silhouette and generous clear space all around; nothing cropped. Single isolated character centered on a truly transparent canvas. No lettering, words, labels, badges, tools, clothing, background, scenery, floor shadow or watermark. Do not imitate any existing brand mascot. Strong coordinated identity that can simplify to a tiny grasshopper app icon.
