## Manti transparent mascot

Tool: built-in `image_gen.imagegen`, transparency enabled. Reference: `assets/brand/concepts/mantis/mantis-concept-board.png`. Output copied to `public/brand/manti-mascot.png`.

Edit the attached approved Mantis Studio identity concept board. Extract only the full-body praying mantis character on the right as a standalone transparent-background mascot asset, called Manti. Preserve the approved character's design, friendly focused expression, triangular green face, expressive dark eyes, long fine antennae, slender body, two folded natural serrated forearms, four supporting legs, leaflike green abdomen, and restrained dimensional illustrative shading as closely as possible. Remove all text, logos, icon tiles, rules, cream background and floor shadow. No redesign, no new props, no pose change, no text, no framing. Center the entire full-body character in a portrait canvas with comfortable transparent margins around both antenna tips and every foot. Actual alpha transparency, not checkerboard or solid fill. This is an in-app mascot asset, not a new concept sheet.
